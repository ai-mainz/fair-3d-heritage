REGISTRATION

Registration is open

FAIR 3D Heritage will take place as an in-person conference at the LUX Pavilion, Hochschule Mainz – University of Applied Sciences, from 14–16 September 2026. The conference programme will also be available via livestream for participants who are unable to join us in Mainz.

Whether you plan to attend the conference in person or would simply like to receive access to the livestream, please register using the conference registration form below. Please note that the number of places for in-person attendance is limited.

The conference registration form also collects information needed for on-site logistics, including attendance days, dietary requirements, participation in the guided city walk, and the social event.

Conference registration

Join the workshops online

On Wednesday, 16 September 2026, selected FAIR 3D Heritage workshops will also offer an interactive online participation option via Zoom.

This is separate from the conference livestream and is intended for participants who would like to actively join the workshop sessions, discussions and activities remotely.

A separate registration is required for online workshop participation. You may register for one, several, or all available workshops. After registration, the Zoom access details and any additional workshop information will be sent to the email address provided shortly before the event.
