Presentation format and submission

Due to the high number of accepted papers, each oral presentation is limited to 15 minutes. This is planned as approximately 10 minutes for the presentation and 5 minutes for questions. If a presentation exceeds 10 minutes, the time available for questions will be shortened accordingly.

Poster presenters are also asked to prepare a short 6-minute spotlight presentation introducing their topic. Poster spotlights will be grouped into thematic sessions and will not include individual questions directly after each presentation. Instead, they will lead into a joint discussion within the respective thematic block.

To ensure smooth transitions between presentations and reliable display both on the main screen and in the livestream, all presentations must be prepared in 16:9 widescreen format. Whenever possible, presentations will be run from the conference computer. PowerPoint (.pptx) and PDF are the preferred file formats.

Connecting your own laptop is possible if technically necessary, but it is not recommended due to the limited transition time between presentations. If you know that you need to use your own device, please inform the organising team in advance.

Please upload your final presentation by Sunday, 13 September 2026 using the form below.

Upload presentation

Please name your presentation file according to the following convention: SessionNumber_Surname_PresentationTitle. If a contribution has several authors, please use the surname of the person who will give the presentation.

Example filename:
03_Ukolov_WebHDP_A_FAIR-Oriented_Publication_Workflow_for_3D_Heritage_Data.pptx

Please use the following session numbers:

  • 01 — Best Documentation Practices
  • 02 — 3D World Wide Web Exploration and Visualisation
  • 03 — Web-based Publication Infrastructures and Preservation Strategies
  • 04 — Poster Session A: Documentation and infrastructures
  • 05 — Education, Outreach and Public Engagement with 3D
  • 06 — Poster Session B: Web visualisation, education and public outreach

If you submit an updated version after your initial upload, please mark it clearly in the filename, for example with _v2 or _FINAL.

If you are unable to upload your presentation by the deadline, please bring the final file on a USB drive and hand it directly to the moderator of your session before the session begins.

Presentations are planned to be streamed live online, and recordings are planned to be published later on the conference website. The relevant consent forms for the recording and publication of presentations will be provided at the conference registration desk.

Poster preparation and display

Physical posters should be prepared in A1 format. Poster presenters should be prepared to take part in the panel discussion and to relate their contribution to the questions and thematic areas described in the Call for Papers. Detailed poster preparation guidelines are available below.

Download poster guidelines

Poster presenters are asked to mount their posters in the designated exhibition area on the ground floor of the LUX Pavilion in the morning upon arrival.

The poster boards are magnetic. The necessary magnets will be provided at the registration desk, so no additional mounting material is required.

Please remember to remove and take your poster with you immediately after the end of the conference. Posters remaining in the exhibition area after the event cannot be stored by the organisers.

Proceedings

Selected authors will be invited to submit a full paper for an open access proceedings volume to be published in the book series Computing in Art and Architecture (CAaA) in arthistoricum.net provided by the Heidelberg University Library.

Only contributions presented in the oral sessions will be considered for the proceedings. These presentations will be evaluated by the session chairs during the conference. Selected contributors will be invited after the conference to prepare a full paper using a layout prepared in cooperation with arthistoricum.net.

Full papers will undergo a double-blind peer review process. Authors will be asked to follow provided formatting guidelines, and further details will be provided together with the invitation. For the double-blind process, author names and affiliations must be removed from the submitted manuscript.

The detailed review workflow and exact deadlines will be announced at a later stage. The proceedings publication is planned for 2027.
